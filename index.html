1. Timoshenko Cryogenic Bimetallic Curvature

The mechanical actuation driving each radiator louver blade stems from the differential thermal contraction between high-expansion brass ($\alpha_1 \approx 19.8 \times 10^{-6}\text{ K}^{-1}$) and low-expansion Invar-36 ($\alpha_2 \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-6}\text{ K}^{-1}$). As temperatures plunge from lunar noon ($380\text{ K}$) to lunar midnight ($80\text{ K}$), the induced radius of curvature $\rho(T)$ is governed by Timoshenko bimetal beam mechanics:

$$\kappa(T) = \frac{1}{\rho(T)} = \frac{6(\alpha_1 - \alpha_2)(T_0 - T)(1+m)^2}{h \left[3(1+m)^2 + (1+mn)\left(m^2 + \frac{1}{mn}\right)\right]}$$

Where $m = t_1 / t_2$ is the thickness ratio of the laminate layers, $n = E_1 / E_2$ is the Young’s modulus ratio, $h = t_1 + t_2$ is the composite strip thickness, and $T_0$ is the unconstrained neutral calibration temperature ($310\text{ K}$). The resultant free angular blade deflection $\theta(T)$ and ideal blocking closing force $F_{\text{ideal}}(T)$ are directly coupled:

$$\theta(T) = \kappa(T) \cdot L_b, \qquad F_{\text{block}}(T) = \frac{3 E_{\text{eq}} I_{\text{eq}}}{2 L_b^2} \cdot (\alpha_1 - \alpha_2)(T_0 - T)$$

2. Sub-20µm Regolith Particle Interlocking

Lunar regolith grit consists of jagged, sub-micron to $20\,\mu\text{m}$ anorthositic/basaltic silicates with sharp aspect ratios, unweathered by atmospheric erosion. When dust infiltrates the louver pivot bearings and perimeter sealing stops, it forms localized force chains governed by Coulomb-Hertz friction with mechanical wedge interlocking:

$$F_{\text{friction}}(\theta) = \sum_{i=1}^{N_p} \left[ \mu_{\text{vac}} \cdot P_i^{2/3} \left(\frac{16 R_i E^*}{9}\right)^{1/3} + \tau_{\text{shear}} \cdot A_{i,\text{contact}} \cdot \cot(\beta_i) \right]$$

In hard vacuum ($10^{-12}\text{ torr}$), adhesive surface energy $\gamma_{\text{ad}}$ elevates the effective friction coefficient from $\mu \approx 0.3$ up to $\mu_{\text{vac}} > 1.8$. If the dust grit density exceeds $\mu_{\text{grit}} \ge 45\text{ mg/cm}^2$, the force required to seat the louver seal exceeds the bimetal strip's closing force budget:

$$F_{\text{net}}(\theta, T) = F_{\text{bimetal}}(T) - F_{\text{seal\_spring}}(\theta) - F_{\text{friction}}(\theta, \mu_{\text{grit}}) \le 0$$

3. Diagnostic State Observer & Jamming Detector

ThermSwitch does not require fragile optical encoders. Instead, thin-film strain gauges sputtered directly onto the bimetallic flexure capture the true reaction bending moment $M(t)$ simultaneously with platinum RTDs measuring strip temperature $T(t)$. The real-time algorithm continuously evaluates the dynamic tangent stiffness:

$$k_{\text{obs}}(t) = \frac{\partial F_{\text{strain}}}{\partial T} \cdot \left(\frac{\partial \theta_{\text{model}}}{\partial T}\right)^{-1}$$

Under nominal closing, $F_{\text{strain}}$ follows the parabolic free-contraction path until reaching the compliant aerogel perimeter seal at $T = 110\text{ K}$. However, when grit binds the hinge, angular motion plateaus ($\dot{\theta} \approx 0$) while bimetallic thermal stress piles up, creating a steep divergence in $\partial F / \partial T$:

$$\text{Jamming Metric } \Lambda(t) = \int_{t-\Delta t}^t \left[ \frac{\mathrm{d}F(t)}{\mathrm{d}T} - \left.\frac{\partial F}{\partial T}\right|_{\text{nominal}} \right] \mathrm{d}t > \Lambda_{\text{crit}} \implies \text{TRIGGER PIEZO}$$

4. High-Frequency Piezo-Acoustic Fluidization

Upon detection of grit binding, localized PZT-5H shear transducers mounted at the louver hinge knuckle excite high-frequency acoustic standing waves. The acoustic shear acceleration $a_{\text{piezo}} = \omega^2 u_0 \sin(\omega t)$ fluidizes the abrasive regolith contact chains:

$$a_{\text{piezo}} = (2\pi \cdot 38.5\text{ kHz})^2 \cdot (2.4\,\mu\text{m}) \approx 142,500\text{ m/s}^2 \approx 14,500\,g_{\text{peak}}$$

This enormous localized acceleration breaks intermolecular London-van der Waals adhesive bonds ($F_{\text{adh}} \sim 10^{-8}\text{ N}$) and micro-welds between sharp silica edges and metal surfaces, allowing the residual bimetallic torque $\tau_{\text{bi}}(T)$ to immediately snap the thermal valve fully shut:

$$F_{\text{inertial}} = m_{\text{dust}} \cdot a_{\text{piezo}} \gg F_{\text{vdW}} + \tau_{\text{shear}} A_{\text{contact}}$$
